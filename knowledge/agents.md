# Frog agents and monthly delivery

## How the agents work

Frog Hub runs seven AI agents. Each has one job taken from the audit, works in the tools Frog already uses, has an autonomy level (prepares and waits for an OK, or acts alone) and a status (live or pilot). Its skills are hub capabilities and automations; everything it sends is logged. Every month the Delivery page shows what was delivered, the hours given back by team, the euros protected, the team pulse and the next agents ranked by impact. Impact units come from the audit when it gives a figure; the others are estimates to confirm with the teams.

## Close-of-service agent

Status: live, since 7 Sept. Works for: Shift managers and GMs. Autonomy: Prepares, the SM confirms and sends. Tools: Zelty, Nepting, Combo, Email.

Closes the service with the SM: reconciles every card payment with its table and server, pre-fills the Manager Report, syncs hours with Combo.

This month: 375 hours given back. Counted from: Manager Reports pre-filled (Audit: 15–30 min saved per service (SM Princess), at least 20 min (Hop House)); Cash-ups reconciled (staff × days) (Audit: 5–12 min per staff per cash-up, 10 cash-ups a night at XVI).
